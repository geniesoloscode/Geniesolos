Systems Engineer, U.S. Federal Government — 10 years and counting. I own the health of a fleet measured in the hundreds of servers, and I refuse to touch any of them one at a time. Everything I do turns into code: provisioning, patching, hardening, evidence collection. If it happens twice, it gets automated once.

Security isn't a scan you pass — it's a property the system holds while nobody's watching. Automation is how you make that property survive contact with reality: consistent baselines, repeatable configuration, and a paper trail that writes itself.
